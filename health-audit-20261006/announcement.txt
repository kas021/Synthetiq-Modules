I've been working on the issue where a movie or episode plays once, then refuses to load when you return or retry.

- X-Stream: fixes for retries getting stuck after earlier failures, while keeping the faster video startup.
- X-Stream also checks titles and seasons more carefully to avoid opening the wrong result.

**Please update both modules:**
1. Open your module list in the app.
2. Check for updates.
3. Update **MovieDB to 0.1.0-beta.12** and **X-Stream to 1.3.3**.

You do not need to reinstall the app.

These changes should make retrying and reopening videos more reliable. Some titles or video qualities may still be unavailable from the providers.
